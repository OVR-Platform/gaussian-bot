# Difix3D+ → ArtiFixer: the evolution

*Audience: our team building 3DGS generative gap-fill at 24 GB. The deliverable is the list of evolutions of ArtiFixer relative to Difix3D+, scoped to what we can act on.*

> **Sourcing convention.** Facts external to this repo (publication venues, dates, author lists, the lab rename) are footnoted to their primary URL. Numbers that appear *only* on a model card or in a third-party profile — not in the paper text — are tagged inline as *card-stated* or *unverified* and consolidated in §5. The same evidential standard is applied to **both** papers' speed/training numbers: where a figure for Difix3D+ has no primary source, it is hedged exactly as ArtiFixer's are.

---

## 1. Lineage

ArtiFixer[^artifixer] and Difix3D+[^difix] come from the **same NVIDIA lab** — the Toronto AI Lab, renamed the **Spatial Intelligence Lab (SIL)** between the two papers.[^silrename] They share **five authors** (Yuxuan Zhang, Jay Zhangjie Wu, Xuanchi Ren, Zan Gojcic, Haithem Turki),[^authors] but ArtiFixer's lead (Riccardo de Lutio) and several collaborators are new, and Difix3D+'s senior PI Sanja Fidler is not in the ArtiFixer author list.[^authors] Calling ArtiFixer "the evolution of Difix3D+" is **partly accurate but easy to overstate**: ArtiFixer cites Difix3D+ as a *baseline it outperforms* (the headline margin is reported as ≈1–3 dB PSNR on observed held-out views — see the metrics caveat in §3/§5), not as a codebase it extends. The architecture is fully swapped — single-step SD-Turbo *image* diffusion + a multi-round progressive 3D loop becomes a Wan2.1-T2V-14B *video* DiT with opacity-mixing init, learned Plücker camera control, and a causal auto-regressive student. The only direct inheritances are the post-processing re-application trick (ArtiFixer3D+, "as in Difix3D+") and the "+" naming nod. **Honest framing: a next-generation successor from the same team — not "Difix3D++", and not a derivative of the Difix3D+ codebase. The hard lineage evidence is (a) shared authors, (b) a cited-baseline relationship, and (c) the named re-application trick; this is consistent with successor/parallel work by the same group, not with one pipeline being grown into the other. Do not imply code reuse.**

## 2. Shared DNA

Both methods pursue the **same research idea, independently re-implemented** — not a shared codebase. The architecture is fully swapped (§1), so what follows is a shared *problem and bake-back pattern*, re-built from scratch in ArtiFixer:

- **Same problem.** Generative correction of NeRF/3DGS artifacts at under-observed novel viewpoints, framed as: *render the degraded novel view → fix it with a diffusion prior → bake the fix back into a 3D reconstruction.*
- **Same high-level pattern (independently re-implemented).** Freeze the base VAE, fine-tune a pretrained diffusion model on **synthesized degraded→clean pairs** whose clean target is a real captured photo, largely mined from **DL3DV**, evaluated on the same benchmark family (**Nerfbusters / DL3DV / Mip-NeRF 360**). Note this is a re-use of *approach*, not procedure: the VAEs, conditioning, distillation, and 3D loop are all different (see §3).
- **Distillation for speed.** Both lean on diffusion distillation so the fixer is cheap enough to run repeatedly (single-step LoRA decoder vs. few-step causal student).
- **Optional reference-view conditioning** and a **final post-processing re-application pass** (ArtiFixer3D+ explicitly re-applies its generator "as in Difix3D+").
- **Same license posture on the part that matters.** Both ship **weights under a restrictive NVIDIA non-commercial license** (license details and the broader 24 GB-surrogate license landscape are reconciled in §3 and §6).

The throughline — stated as an *observed difference in design choices*, not a claim that one design was edited into the other: Difix3D+ has no consistency or camera-awareness of its own and *delegates both to the 3D loop*; ArtiFixer's independent design pulls those responsibilities *into the model*. Most differences below follow from that single design decision.

## 4. The most important evolutions (and the Difix3D+ limitation each addresses)

### 4.1 Single-step image model → 14B video model
**Difix3D+ limitation:** a 2D SD-Turbo UNet has no notion of time or multiple views — it sees one degraded frame and emits one cleaned frame. It has no generative capacity to invent coherent content across a sequence, only to *denoise* an existing one. (Its VAE is a per-frame image autoencoder with decoder skip-surgery — see the Latent/VAE row.)
**ArtiFixer:** swaps the backbone for **Wan2.1-T2V-14B**, a video diffusion transformer with a **3D causal video VAE** and native spatio-temporal priors. This is the enabling move — every other evolution below is downstream of having a model that natively reasons over a clip rather than a frame.

### 4.2 Per-frame → temporally-consistent video
**Difix3D+ limitation:** independent single-frame fixes are *not* mutually consistent (the paper's own Fig. 8 shows cross-view inconsistency from direct per-frame application). The 2D model produces flicker if used standalone.
**ArtiFixer:** generates multi-frame clips jointly (clip length is an operational parameter — the "81-frame" figure is card/profile-stated, §5), so frames are coherent by construction. The video backbone is the consistency mechanism, not an afterthought.

### 4.3 Reliance on the progressive 3D loop for consistency → intrinsic video consistency
**Difix3D+ limitation:** because the 2D model is inconsistent on its own, consistency is *bought* by distilling every fix back into the single shared 3D representation, which acts as a multi-view fusion bottleneck. That is a heavy, reconstruction-time procedure (many rounds, ≈60k iters).
**ArtiFixer:** consistency comes from three reinforcing in-model signals (video DiT + strong per-frame conditioning + causal KV cache). Because the model is *already* consistent, the 3D step can collapse from a multi-round crawl to a **single-pass generate-then-fit**. This is the single largest workflow simplification.

### 4.4 Reference-view conditioning → opacity-mixing latent init
**Difix3D+ limitation:** the only "where am I unobserved" signal is implicit — it arrives baked into the degraded render. With nothing telling the model a region is *empty*, image-mapping fixers mode-collapse when all input pixels are black; `difix_ref` quality also degrades when no good nearest reference exists.
**ArtiFixer:** the rendered **opacity map** drives a latent init **`z_mix = O_z·z_deg + (1−O_z)·ε`**, where **`O_z` is the opacity downsampled (max-pooled) to *latent* resolution** — this pooling is load-bearing and must not be skipped when implementing from this doc, because the image VAE compresses ~8× spatially (and Wan's video VAE compresses temporally too). High-opacity (observed) tokens stay faithful to the degraded latent; opacity≈0 (empty) tokens become **pure noise**, so the model is fully generative exactly where it must invent. This explicitly partitions cleanup-vs-hallucination and is the direct cure for empty-region mode collapse. **It is also backbone-agnostic and nearly free** — see §6. (See §4.6 for the related-but-separate point that the *single-step* path in observed regions is Difix3D+'s inherited capability, merely *routed* by opacity here.)

### 4.5 Implicit pose → learned camera control
**Difix3D+ limitation:** pose lives entirely in the renderer. The 2D model is pose-agnostic; it has no way to *place* invented content at a commanded camera. Fine for re-rendering existing geometry, useless for empty volume.
**ArtiFixer:** per-pixel **Plücker raymaps** R=(d, o×d) are injected additively in every transformer block (bypassing the VAE), and reference views carry **relative** pose via PRoPE in cross-attention. The model can now generate correctly-placed content at an arbitrary commanded pose, even where nothing was observed.

### 4.6 Single-step → DMD few-step causal-AR streaming student
**Difix3D+ limitation:** the single-step trick keeps *one* frame cheap, but offers no path to many consistent frames quickly — and there is no streaming/long-horizon mechanism at all.
**ArtiFixer:** distills the bidirectional teacher into a **few-step (N≈4) causal auto-regressive student** via Self-Forcing-style DMD (block-causal mask + Diffusion-Forcing), with a **rolling KV cache** so each chunk attends to prior frames. This is what makes 14B-scale generation of many consistent frames in a single pass tractable and streamable. **Attribution note:** the *single-step-in-observed-regions* capability is **inherited from Difix3D+**, not invented here — opacity-mixing (§4.4) decides *where* the cheap single-step path applies; the cheap path itself is the Difix3D+ legacy. (Mechanism names — Self-Forcing, Diffusion-Forcing, rolling KV — are profile-stated and not primary-sourced; §5.)

### 4.7 Small near-camera holes → large under-observed regions / long trajectories
**Difix3D+ limitation:** the progressive small-step schedule exists *precisely because* the model degrades on heavily-corrupted, far-from-reference renders. It covers far/long regimes only by crawling there incrementally; it cannot jump.
**ArtiFixer:** the opacity-mixing partition (§4.4) plus the causal KV student (§4.6) target the far/empty/long regime head-on — reported success in **completely unobserved** regions and arbitrary-length trajectories. The often-quoted ≈1–3 dB PSNR margin is **measured on held-out observed views**, not on the empty-volume regime (PSNR has no ground truth there); the empty-region win rests on perceptual/distributional evidence, not PSNR (see the Evaluation row). This is the regime Difix3D+'s near-camera loop does not natively cover.

## 5. Caveats (claims the verification did NOT fully support — do not assert as fact)

- **Lineage facts — partly confirmed (2026-06-29).** The ArtiFixer **arXiv ID `2603.00492`**, its title, the **Wan2.1-T2V-14B** base, the **~16.9 B params**, the **NVIDIA OneWay Noncommercial + Wan Apache-2.0** license, and the **Toronto AI Lab → Spatial Intelligence Lab** rename were independently re-fetched and confirmed (arXiv listing, NVIDIA SIL project page, HF model card released 2026-06-04). **Still asserted-not-confirmed:** the **SIGGRAPH 2026** venue, the CVPR 2025 Oral / Best-Paper-finalist status, the exact five-author overlap, de Lutio as lead, and Fidler's absence — read these from each paper's author block (footnotes in §1) before relying on them.
- **The ≈1–3 dB PSNR margin is observed-region-only and uncited at page/table level.** It is repeated across §1/§3/§4 *as a single figure*; it cannot describe the empty-region regime (no ground truth → PSNR undefined there). Do not use it as evidence for hallucination quality.
- **Difix3D+ speed/latency ("~76 ms / A100", ">10× faster than multi-step") — UNVERIFIED.** Not in any verified in-repo material. Held to the **same** standard as ArtiFixer's FPS figures below; the earlier asymmetry (Difix3D+ asserted as fact, ArtiFixer quarantined) is corrected.
- **ArtiFixer FPS figures (~8.36 FPS / ~268 FPS, ~70× vs teacher) — UNVERIFIED.** The paper states only that inference "reaches interactive rates" but is "significantly slower than direct rendering." The qualitative claim is the safe one.
- **ArtiFixer "16.9B" — CONFIRMED (card-stated, re-fetched 2026-06-29).** The HF model card explicitly states "~16.9 billion trainable parameters", base **Wan2.1-T2V-14B**, dual **NVIDIA OneWay Noncommercial + Wan Apache-2.0** license. **"~15,000 GPU-hours", "128× H100", and the inference-GPU list remain UNVERIFIED** — the paper/card name only the backbone and give no total GPU-hours or hardware list; §6's "out of reach" bullet hedges these rather than restating them as fact.
- **ArtiFixer "81-frame clips" / "hundreds of views in a single pass" — UNVERIFIED hard numbers.** Card/profile-stated; the only verified statement is the qualitative "reaches interactive rates / significantly slower than direct rendering." Hedged wherever it appears.
- **ArtiFixer distillation mechanism names ("N≈4 causal AR student", "Self-Forcing DMD", "Diffusion-Forcing", "rolling KV cache", "sub-4 / 1-step in observed regions") — profile-stated, not primary-cited.** Given §5 already refutes the pose-distance formula and the FPS numbers *from the same profile source*, these names are reported at **Med** confidence, not blanket High.
- **ArtiFixer pose-distance formula `d = θ/π + λ_t·‖t‖/r̄` — REFUTED.** The paper uses `d = ‖R_i − R_j‖_F + ‖t_i − t_j‖_2` (Frobenius + L2), with no θ/π, no λ_t, no scene-radius normalization. The corrected Frobenius+L2 form is what §6 recommends for pair-mining.
- **`difix_ref` reference mechanism — PARTLY RIGHT (was previously called REFUTED; corrected).** *Both* descriptions are accurate at different stages: the released code **does** concatenate the inputs on the **batch dim** (`pipeline_difix.py:1025` → `torch.cat([image, ref_image], dim=0)`), and the custom UNet **then** creates a **view dimension internally** via einops rearrange `(b v) n d -> b (v n) d` with `num_views=2` for the joint self-attention (`unet_2d_condition.py:76`/`:128`). So inputs are batch-dim concatenated, then reshaped to a view dim *inside* `attn1`. The in-repo docstring (`fillers/diffusion.py`) agrees: the pipeline "batches `[image, ref_image]`" and the "reference-mixing self-attention … lets the degraded view borrow appearance." Do **not** call the batch-dim description false.
- **Opacity-mixing must pool opacity to latent resolution.** The correct form (per `splat-enhancement-study.md`) is `z_mix = O_z·z_deg + (1−O_z)·ε` with **`O_z` = opacity max-pooled to latent resolution**, not the pixel-space `O`. Implementing with raw pixel-space opacity against an ~8×-downsampled latent is a silent fidelity bug.
- **"Off-trajectory" as a primary ArtiFixer claim — NOT paper-supported.** ArtiFixer's emphasis is scalability ("many frames in a single pass") and *under-observed* regions; "long off-trajectory paths" is not in the abstract/intro. The black-pixel mode-collapse is attributed by ArtiFixer to **image** fixers, not bidirectional ones.
- **"Very long trajectories may drift" — profile-author hedge, NOT a paper claim.** The paper claims conditioning is sufficient and cites latency, not drift, as the limitation.
- **Difix3D+ specifics that are illustrative, not paper-exact:** "~29 progressive rounds" and `shift_poses(distance=0.5)` come from the released gsplat trainer; the *paper* says only "after every ≈1.5k iterations" and "slightly perturb" with no numeric 0.5. The fixed prompt `"remove degradation"` is a **config default** (`diffusion.py`), and the standalone probe runs an empty prompt; the single-step model is effectively prompt-insensitive — not a paper-load-bearing fact. The "transforms-style JSON" inference format is unconfirmed.
- **Difix3D+ 2D-fixer training specifics — UNSOURCED, and must not be conflated with the 3D-distill loss.** Pair counts (~80k DL3DV / ~100k automotive), shift range (1–6 m), and the 2D-fixer loss (`L2 + LPIPS + Gram`) have no in-repo source. They describe the **2D image-fixer** stage; the **3D-distillation** stage uses `0.8·L1 + 0.2·(1−SSIM)` (per `difix-gap-artifixer-24gb.md`). The two are different losses for different stages.
- **Difix3D+ parameter count and native resolution** are not published on its model cards (576×1024 is paper/training-data-cited, not restated in the released pipeline).
- **License strings for both papers are asserted without a source link** and should be confirmed against the actual HF/repo license files before any productization decision (see §6 for how this interacts with the SEVA-NonCommercial 24 GB surrogate).

## 6. Implications for our 24 GB pipeline

### Borrow now — opacity-mixing latent init (highest ROI)
The single most transferable idea is **`z_mix = O_z·z_deg + (1−O_z)·ε`**. It is **backbone-agnostic and nearly free**: it needs only the rendered opacity map we already produce from our 3DGS, and it works as a *latent-init policy* on top of whatever diffusion fixer we run. **Implementation must pool opacity to latent resolution first** (`O_z` = max-pool of pixel opacity to the latent grid — see §5); using pixel-space opacity against a downsampled latent is a silent bug. Concrete uses:
- Drive our filler's init with `O_z` so observed tokens start from the faithful degraded latent (cheap, few-step cleanup) and empty tokens start from pure noise (full generation).
- Use `O_z` as an explicit per-region step-count router: clamp observed regions toward the cheap single-step path, spend the step budget on the empty tokens that need it. **Note the single-step capability in observed regions is the Difix3D+ inheritance** (§4.6) — opacity-mixing routes *where* it applies, it does not invent it; so this win is available to us only insofar as our backbone supports a cheap single/low-step mode.

### Also worth borrowing (cheap, model-class-independent)
- **Plücker raymap conditioning** injected additively per block, VAE-bypassing — gives our smaller backbone explicit camera control without retraining the VAE. Prerequisite for placing invented content at a commanded pose, which we need for any gap-fill far from a camera.
- **Single-pass generate-then-fit** as the 3D integration target. If our filler is multi-view consistent enough, fit a fresh reconstruction from generated targets + real anchors rather than running a multi-round Difix-style crawl — far cheaper in reconstruction time and a better fit for the **cheap per-scene loop** our 200k-scene ambition needs (we explicitly *do not* want a universal feed-forward model — see the per-scene row in §3).
- **Sparse-view degradation pair-mining** with the **corrected Frobenius+L2 pose-distance** clustering (`d = ‖R_i−R_j‖_F + ‖t_i−t_j‖_2`; *not* the refuted θ/π form, §5) — a clean way to manufacture our own degraded→clean training pairs from any DL3DV-style capture.

### Out of reach at 24 GB (do not plan around these)
- **The 14B Wan2.1 teacher.** Its inference is 80 GB-class; there is no published single-GPU/low-VRAM path. We must use a **smaller diffusion backbone** as a surrogate. Its training cost (the ~15k-GPU-hour / 128× H100 figures are **unverified**, §5) is irrelevant to us either way.
- **The AR streaming student as released.** Built on the 14B teacher. We can adopt the *pattern* (few-step causal AR + rolling KV cache for long trajectories) on a smaller backbone, but the released student weights are non-commercial and too large.
- **Productizing any NVIDIA weights.** ArtiFixer code is Apache-2.0 but its weights are non-commercial — for anything shippable we build on the Apache-2.0 base (Wan2.1) or our own surrogate, never the released checkpoint. **License reconciliation:** our companion 24 GB scoping doc (`difix-gap-artifixer-24gb.md`) lands on **SEVA / Stable Virtual Camera (1.3 B)** as the realistic 24 GB surrogate, but SEVA is itself **NonCommercial** — so the productization blocker is *not* unique to ArtiFixer; both the ArtiFixer checkpoint and the leading 24 GB surrogate are non-commercial. A commercially-clean ship requires either the Apache-2.0 base re-finetuned, an OpenRAIL-class inpainting backbone, or our own trained surrogate.

### The one regime where ArtiFixer's design genuinely beats Difix3D+
**Large gaps far from any camera, combined with moving geometry / long trajectories.** Difix3D+ structurally cannot fill truly empty volume in one shot — its progressive crawl keeps inputs in-distribution and it mode-collapses on all-black input. ArtiFixer's opacity-partitioned generation + learned camera control + causal long-horizon student are purpose-built for exactly this case. **Caveat on "beats":** in this regime there is no ground truth, so the advantage is *perceptual/structural plausibility*, not a PSNR number (§5). **Our action:** target our surrogate at *this* regime specifically. For small near-camera residual artifacts, a Difix-style single-step image fixer remains the cheaper, 24 GB-native choice — reserve the heavier ArtiFixer-style machinery (opacity-mixing init + raymap control + single-pass fit) for the large, far-from-camera, under-observed gaps where it is the only design that works.
